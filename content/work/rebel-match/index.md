## Nobody should face a hard change alone

People in the [Corporate Rebels](https://www.corporate-rebels.com) network are
changing how their organizations work. Each of them brings challenges from
their own organization. And somewhere in the same network is someone facing
the same thing right now — or someone who has already solved it.

Rebel Match exists so that those people find each other. Not in a directory,
not by luck at the coffee bar, but in the few minutes of a conference break.

> Find the peers who face your challenge — or have already solved it.

## What it leads to

A member writes a challenge in their own words, and Rebel Match points them to
three kinds of help:

- **Same boat** — peers who face the same thing right now, to work through it
  together.
- **Been there** — peers who have solved it and can share their experience.
- **Case studies** — stories from organizations that changed it.

It works the other way too. Members can **offer help**: look through other
people's challenges and say "I'm in the same boat" or "I can share
experience". Every challenge belongs to one of the eight Corporate Rebels
trends — from _Profit_ to _Purpose & Values_, from _Hierarchical Pyramid_ to
_Network of Teams_ — so the conversation starts from shared ground.

## Trust comes first

Meeting someone should never mean being exposed. Connecting is a **double
yes**: the other person is asked, and only when both agree are email addresses
exchanged. Until then, nobody can see anyone's contact details.

## Made for a moment

Rebel Match was built for the Corporate Rebels summit on 8 November 2026,
where its beta launched. Attendees scan a QR code with their phone, and the
aim is to get from that scan to being set up in **under two minutes** — so the
break is spent meeting people, not filling in forms.

## Who made it

Pascal Dulex brought the idea, the design and the community; Andy Moesch the
engineering; Ivo Pejakovic the user experience and the testing. Around them
are the Rebel Match contributors and the Corporate Rebels community, who bring
the challenges, the experience and the connections.

## Open for everyone

Rebel Match is open source under the MIT license. Anyone can read how it
works, learn from it, or build on it for their own community.
