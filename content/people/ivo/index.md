## Short bio

I am an ICT business analyst and IT project lead. I came to IT from the shop
floor, moving from production and planning to processes, systems and then
organisation design. At a Swiss technology manufacturer, I first helped my own
team become self-organised and then supported the whole company through the
same change. I am regularly invited to speak and run workshops on
self-organisation, and at xigo I offer this experience pro bono, as a place to
try things out rather than as a consulting business.

## What I can help with

- **Roles and self-organisation:** helps volunteer teams and boards agree who
  decides what, so the work doesn’t depend on a single person.
- **Consent decision-making:** gives committees and communities a quick and
  fair way to decide without endless discussion.
- **Workshop and strategy facilitation:** helps a foundation or association
  agree on a shared direction and turn it into working agreements.
- **Process and system selection:** helps a non-profit choose tools that fit
  how it works, and redesign processes instead of copying old ones.
- **Fair pay and development processes:** helps organisations with staff make
  pay decisions more transparent and fair.
- **Learning and feedback formats** (open space, fishbowls, events about
  mistakes): help communities learn together and raise concerns early.
- **Events, fundraising and community communication:** helps events and causes
  with organising, announcements and fundraising events.
- **User testing and pilots:** helps community projects test an app or service
  with real users before launch.
