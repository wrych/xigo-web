## Short bio

I am a product leader with an engineering background. I started out as a
field systems engineer for high-tech instruments, moved into product and
software ownership, and went on to lead an agile transformation and build a
data team. Most of my work has been about bridging business and engineering:
turning what people need into software that works, and helping teams organise
themselves around it. At xigo I offer this experience pro bono, as a place to
try things out rather than as a consulting business.

## What I can help with

- **Product ownership for community software:** helps a volunteer project turn
  ideas into a clear backlog, decide what matters most and ship it in small
  steps.
- **User research and testing:** helps a non-profit watch how its work really
  happens, find the friction and test a solution with real users before it is
  rolled out.
- **Choosing and replacing systems:** helps an organisation replace old tools
  without copying old processes, and keep scope and budget under control.
- **Data and reporting:** helps a foundation or association get the numbers it
  needs out of its own data, with open-source tools it can keep running.
- **AI, with care:** helps a team find out whether AI actually helps with a
  problem, by building a small prototype before committing to anything.
- **Agile ways of working:** helps teams set up planning, reviews and
  retrospectives that fit them, not a textbook.
- **Software quality:** helps volunteer developers add tests and automated
  checks, so a project keeps working when people come and go.
- **Translating between people and technology:** helps boards and volunteers
  talk with developers and suppliers, so both sides understand what is decided.
